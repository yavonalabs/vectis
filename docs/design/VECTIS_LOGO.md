# Vectis logo: the lever V

The mark combines the initial V with a triangular fulcrum. Its longer violet arm suggests upward movement; rounded terminals keep it approachable. The concept connects the name's meaning, lever, to helping operators complete configured tasks with less effort.

Assets live in `docs/site/assets/`: primary, light-on-dark, single-color and favicon SVGs. They are original vector geometry, scalable without rasterization. The preview at `docs/site/logo.html` shows the wordmark, meanings and compact sizes. The product page uses the primary mark and favicon. The approved identity is also applied to the Studio sidebar, mobile header, sample login and browser favicon. Application copies live in `vectis-core/src/main/resources/static/vectis-assets/`; keep them identical to the site assets when updating the mark.

Use the primary mark on ivory or white; use the light variant on forest green. Preserve the proportions and leave at least the triangle's width as clear space. Use the favicon variant below 32px. Do not animate or decorate this mark in operation confirmations. The adjacent wordmark currently uses the page's system font; the standalone marks have no font dependency.

Palette: forest `#173f32`, violet `#713091`, ivory `#f7f8f2`, light lilac `#dbc2ee`. This is a proposed identity, not a trademark clearance or a claim of exclusivity.
